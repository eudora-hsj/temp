## Contents

- **Site-level annual emissions**: 41 sites, 2005–2024, Scope 1 and Scope 2 reported separately
- **Original values and adjustments**: every harmonisation retains the pre-adjustment figure, the delta and the rule code
- **Conversion rule documentation**: the three-layer ruleset with the reasoning behind each judgement
- **Unresolved disagreements**: seven items on which the three teams did not converge, with each position stated
- **Known limitations**: where gaps fall, which fields are given as ranges, and which analyses are affected

## Why the original values are retained

Most harmonised datasets publish only the result. Retaining originals and deltas lets researchers who disagree with our rules work back to the starting point and recompute under their own.

The cost is roughly three times the file size and a more complex field structure. We consider that a fair trade: **a set of harmonisation rules that cannot be inspected is not, methodologically, much better than the unharmonised statistics it replaces.**

## Limitations on use

Fields touching undisclosed commercial detail are given as ranges — 214 records in total. These introduce uncertainty at site level but have limited effect on national aggregates (estimated ±1.2%).

Version 2.1 corrects an on-site generation attribution error affecting three Japanese sites for 2011–2013, reported by an external researcher. The revision log is included.
