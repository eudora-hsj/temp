Ethylene plants in the three countries run comparable processes with comparable emission profiles. Yet plotted on the same axes, their decarbonization curves diverge sharply. The difference lies not in technology but in how carbon is priced, where inventory boundaries are drawn, and who absorbs the cost of transition.
