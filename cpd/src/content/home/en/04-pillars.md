eyebrow: Research strands
title: Three strands that must cite each other
subtitle: The strands are not parallel workstreams. Without the boundary harmonisation of the first, the policy comparison in the second has nothing to stand on.
items:
  - title: Inventory boundaries and comparability
    desc: The three countries classify the petrochemical sector differently, assign on-site generation to different sectors, and calculate Scope 2 on different bases. This strand builds a traceable set of conversion rules placing all 41 sites on one coordinate system.
  - title: Policy instruments and investment decisions
    desc: Tracking how carbon prices, subsidies and transition funds shift the timing of equipment replacement. The object of study is realised capital expenditure, not policy announcements.
  - title: The social cost of transition
    desc: Pollution exposure in host communities, retraining pathways for workers, and local fiscal dependence. Comparisons that omit these items systematically understate the cost of certain pathways.
