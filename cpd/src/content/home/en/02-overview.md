CPD is run jointly by teams in Taiwan, the United Kingdom and Japan, covering the principal naphtha cracking and derivative chemical sites in each country. The project does not rank technology pathways. It addresses a prior question: whether decarbonization figures built on different statistical boundaries can be compared at all.

The first year was spent almost entirely on harmonising data definitions. That work reads like groundwork, but it is the project's main scholarly contribution.
