Team composition deliberately keeps headcount and decision weight balanced across the three countries. This guards against a familiar failure in comparative work: a team from one country leading the study and treating its national conventions as the default baseline. Every harmonisation rule requires agreement from all three teams, and any dissent is recorded in the methodology document.
